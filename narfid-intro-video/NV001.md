# NV-001 — آخر خمس دقائق في الوردية

37 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Educational film for a club's operations and reception team. Brief source: `lib/marketing/video-briefs.ts`, read 11 November 2026; live board revision 4 before production start.

## Direction

An approaching shift change is expressed through a physical clock. A pending reservation stays visible after the clock stops. Three pieces of information are written onto a handoff sheet. Two distinct cases move to the next shift with explicit follow-up times. A three-item challenge makes the advice actionable. The original Arabic logo closes the film.

These are original editorial motion graphics, not software screenshots or a claim that a specific handoff feature exists in the platform. All roles, cases and times are fictional. No reference-system connection, customer data or external visual assets are used. Original logo and bundled Tajawal fonts come from the existing project. Brand colors follow `lib/brand.ts`; the referenced UI-system skill was absent when searched.
